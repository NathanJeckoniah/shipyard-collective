What this is

Shipyard Collective is a community built around one idea: engineers get better by contributing to real infrastructure work and talking to other people doing the same thing. No growth hacks, no engagement bait — just a forum where DevOps and platform engineers discuss pipelines, infrastructure, incident war stories, and open-source contributions, moderated closely enough to stay useful.

What we do

Contribute to open source

Members put real commits into real DevOps and infrastructure tooling — not tutorials, actual projects people depend on.

Discuss, in depth

Threads on pipelines, outages, tools and trade-offs — the kind of conversation that doesn't fit in a group chat.

Moderated, on purpose

Low noise, high signal. Moderators keep the forum sharp instead of letting it dissolve into chatter.
